# Design context

- Scope: experimental architecture for camera-based picking and RL job sequencing in the SO-ARM101 work cell.
- Audience: the project team and experiment reviewers.
- Language: English explanations; code identifiers and units retain their original spelling.
- Original experiments.ipynb: one WOWROBO SO-ARM101 follower, two input stations with separate output spots, 480-second shifts, eight candidate jobs, normal/careful handling, masked PPO, masked double dueling DQN, six dispatch rules.
- User requirement: adapt the simulation and camera-based picking pipeline to this experiment; inspect both LeHome repositories.
- Current status: the camera experiment is implemented in vision_workcell_experiments.ipynb. Local checks use synthetic measurements; Isaac Sim physics execution remains unverified.
- Hardware identified by the user: WOWROBO SO-ARM101. The manufacturer's camera-included Package 2/3 lists one camera; its SO-ARM100/101 camera product lists 2MP, 30 FPS, and a 3m USB cable. The kit page does not identify the bundled camera sensor or exact capture modes.
- Camera design: use the supplied camera as a single RGB image input. The manufacturer's published specification does not list depth measurement. Do not expose simulated depth to this policy. The simulation mounts the camera under the fixed gripper body after wrist_roll. Its transform changes with the arm. The mount and optical settings require real-camera calibration before hardware use.
- Unpublished camera details: exact capture resolutions/FPS combinations, FOV, intrinsics, distortion, focus, and latency. Confirm through device mode enumeration and calibration before freezing simulator parameters. 640×480 was a proposed model input, not the verified hardware resolution.
- Current scheduler: 79 inputs, 16 actions, nine Phase 2 baselines, and the five Phase 2 score terms. Camera confidence controls action availability.
- The animated comparison in docs/phase2-vs-vision-workcell.html shows a schematic decision, not measured robot performance.
